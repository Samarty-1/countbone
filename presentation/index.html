How it works

Film, count, review, prove.

Every module runs on the same four steps. What changes is where the expected number comes from: the ERP book, a purchase order or a planogram.

01 · Phone

Film

Walk the bay. The app coaches pace, tilt and light while you record.

 02 · Server

Count

Each item is detected, tracked across frames and matched to a product taught from photos.

 03 · Person

Review

Anything uncertain is flagged for a person, never reported as certain. One swipe settles it.

 04 · Finance

Prove

Corrections post to your ERP with a signed evidence pack behind each one.

What's proven

The pilot

Four weeks, two or three aisles.

Week 0

Set up

Half a day on site: bay labels, photos of your 20–50 fastest movers, accounts and the app.

Week 1

Calibrate

Count by video and by hand, side by side. Tune approval limits with your finance lead.

Weeks 2–3

Run

Video only. Recount tasks close the loop, managers approve, your ERP is connected.

Week 4

Review

A report on time per bay, accuracy against hand counts, variance found and what it was worth.
